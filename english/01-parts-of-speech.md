# English 1 — Parts of Speech

**Official line:** Parts of speech  
**Time:** 55 minutes  

---

## Today's goal

Name all **8** parts of speech, give one example each, and spot them in a sentence.

---

## 1. The eight parts

| Part | Job | Examples |
|------|-----|----------|
| **Noun** | Name of a person, place, thing, or idea | Ravi, Bengaluru, book, honesty |
| **Pronoun** | Stands in place of a noun | I, you, he, she, it, we, they, this, who |
| **Adjective** | Describes a noun / pronoun | tall, seven, Kannada, honest |
| **Verb** | Action or state | teach, is, have, write |
| **Adverb** | Describes a verb, adjective, or adverb | slowly, very, yesterday, here |
| **Preposition** | Shows relation (place, time, etc.) | in, on, at, of, for, with |
| **Conjunction** | Joins words or clauses | and, but, or, because, although |
| **Interjection** | Sudden feeling | Oh! Alas! Hurrah! Wow! |

Some books add **articles** (a, an, the) as a separate class. In many papers they are a kind of **adjective / determiner**.

---

## 2. Noun — types (MCQ)

| Type | Meaning | Example |
|------|---------|---------|
| Proper | Special name (capital) | Karnataka, Ganga |
| Common | General name | teacher, river |
| Collective | Group as one | class, flock, jury |
| Abstract | Idea / quality | truth, childhood |
| Material | Substance | gold, rice, water |
| Countable | Can count | book, books |
| Uncountable | Cannot count | milk, advice |

**Number:** singular / plural (`child → children`).  
**Gender:** masculine, feminine, common, neuter.  
**Case:** nominative (subject), objective (object), possessive (`Ravi’s`).

---

## 3. Pronoun — types

Personal (`I, him`), possessive (`mine, yours`), reflexive (`myself`), demonstrative (`this, those`), interrogative (`who, what`), relative (`who, which, that`), indefinite (`someone, all`).

**Tip:** A pronoun must match its noun in number and person (*The students… they*).

---

## 4. Adjective — types

Quality (`honest`), quantity (`some, much`), number (`five, many`), demonstrative (`this, those`), interrogative (`which`), possessive (`my, their`).

**Order (short):** opinion → size → age → colour → noun  
`a nice small old red box`

**Articles:** **a** (consonant sound), **an** (vowel sound: *an hour, an MBA*), **the** (known / unique / superlative).

---

## 5. The others in one box

- **Verb** — full chapters 2 and 3.  
- **Adverb** — how / when / where / how much: *quickly, now, there, very*.  
- **Preposition** — *interested **in**, good **at**, consist **of**, prefer A **to** B, since 2020, for two years*.  
- **Conjunction** — coordinating (*and, but, or*); subordinating (*because, if, when*); correlative (*either…or, neither…nor*).  
- **Interjection** — followed by ! in writing.

---

## 6. Same word, different part (exam trap)

| Word | As noun | As verb | As adjective |
|------|---------|---------|--------------|
| book | a book | book a ticket | — |
| light | a light | light a lamp | a light bag |
| well | a well | — | — |
| well (adv) | — | — | — (*She sings well*) |

*Fast* can be adjective or adverb. *After* can be preposition or conjunction.

---

## 7. Spotting drill

*The honest teacher quickly taught them in the lab.*

- The — article  
- honest — adjective  
- teacher — noun  
- quickly — adverb  
- taught — verb  
- them — pronoun  
- in — preposition  
- the lab — article + noun

## 8. MCQs

**1.** Honesty is a / an:  
a) Proper noun  b) Abstract noun  c) Verb  d) Interjection  

**2.** *She* is a:  
a) Noun  b) Pronoun  c) Adverb  d) Preposition  

**3.** *Very* in “very tall” is:  
a) Adjective  b) Adverb  c) Verb  d) Noun  

**4.** *Because* is a:  
a) Preposition  b) Conjunction  c) Interjection  d) Article  

**5.** We use **an** before:  
a) university (yu…)  b) hour  c) book  d) useful idea (yu…)  

**6.** *Class* in “the class is noisy” is often:  
a) Abstract  b) Collective  c) Material  d) Pronoun  

**7.** Oh! is:  
a) Conjunction  b) Interjection  c) Preposition  d) Modal  

**8.** *On* in “on the table” is:  
a) Adverb always  b) Preposition  c) Noun  d) Verb  

### Answers

1-b, 2-b, 3-b, 4-b, 5-b, 6-b, 7-b, 8-b

## Quick revision

8 parts: noun, pronoun, adjective, verb, adverb, preposition, conjunction, interjection. Articles a/an/the. Same word can change class.
