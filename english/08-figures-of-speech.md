# English 8 — Figures of Speech

**Official line:** Figures of speech  
**Time:** 50 minutes  

---

## Today's goal

Name the figure, give a school-level example, and pick it in a line of poetry.

---

## 1. What it is

> A **figure of speech** is a special use of words to make language **vivid**, not only literal.

---

## 2. Must-know list

| Figure | Meaning | Example |
|--------|---------|---------|
| **Simile** | Compare using *like* or *as* | He is **as brave as a lion**. She runs **like the wind**. |
| **Metaphor** | Compare **without** like/as; says it *is* | He is a **lion** in the field. The classroom was a **zoo**. |
| **Personification** | Give human qualities to non-human | The **flowers danced**. Time **steals** our days. |
| **Hyperbole** | Extreme exaggeration | I have told you **a thousand times**. |
| **Alliteration** | Same consonant sound at the start | **Peter Piper picked**. **Full fathom five**. |
| **Onomatopoeia** | Word that sounds like the noise | *buzz, hiss, bang, splash, cuckoo* |
| **Oxymoron** | Two opposite words together | *living death, deafening silence, bitter sweet* |
| **Irony** | Say the opposite of what is meant / unexpected | “Beautiful weather!” (in a storm). A fire station burns. |
| **Apostrophe** | Address the absent / dead / object | **O Death!** *O Solitude!* |
| **Metonymy** | Name related thing instead | **The Crown** (= king). **The stage** (= acting). |
| **Synecdoche** | Part for whole (or whole for part) | **All hands** on deck. **India** won (the team). |
| **Euphemism** | Soft word for a hard fact | *passed away* (= died) |
| **Pun** | Play on two meanings | A **grave** matter (serious / tomb). |
| **Antithesis** | Opposite ideas in balance | *United we stand, divided we fall.* |
| **Repetition** | Repeat for force | *Alone, alone, all, all alone* |
| **Transferred epithet** | Adjective shifted to another noun | *sleepless night* (the person is sleepless) |

---

## 3. Simile vs metaphor (always asked)

| Simile | Metaphor |
|--------|----------|
| *like / as* | No like/as |
| He fights **like** a tiger | He is a tiger |

---

## 4. Poetry line drill

- “The moon was a ghostly galleon” — **metaphor**  
- “O wild West Wind” — **apostrophe** (+ personification)  
- “I wandered lonely as a cloud” — **simile**  
- “The wind whispered” — **personification**  
- “Ten thousand saw I at a glance” — **hyperbole**

---

## 5. MCQs

**1.** “He is as cunning as a fox” is:  
a) Metaphor  b) Simile  c) Irony  d) Pun  

**2.** “The sea is a hungry dog” is:  
a) Simile  b) Metaphor  c) Oxymoron  d) Tense  

**3.** “Buzz” is:  
a) Hyperbole  b) Onomatopoeia  c) Metonymy  d) Concord  

**4.** “Deafening silence” is:  
a) Simile  b) Oxymoron  c) Apostrophe  d) Article  

**5.** “O Captain! My Captain!” is mainly:  
a) Pun  b) Apostrophe  c) Only tense  d) Cluster  

**6.** “All hands on deck” is:  
a) Synecdoche  b) Only adverb  c) Modal  d) Passive  

**7.** Same starting sound: “wild west wind” is:  
a) Irony  b) Alliteration  c) Suffix  d) Clause  

**8.** “I could sleep for a year” is:  
a) Litotes  b) Hyperbole  c) Concord  d) ISP  

### Answers

1-b, 2-b, 3-b, 4-b, 5-b, 6-a, 7-b, 8-b

---

## Quick revision

Simile = like/as. Metaphor = is. Personification = human to thing. Hyperbole = exaggerate. Alliteration = same start sound. Oxymoron = opposite pair.
