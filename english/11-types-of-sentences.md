# English 11 — Types of Sentences (Seven Types)

**Official line:** Types of sentences (seven types)  
**Time:** 50 minutes  

---

## Today's goal

Indian TRT books often list **seven types by meaning**. Learn them and how to **transform** one into another.

---

## 1. The seven types (by meaning / function)

| # | Type | Job | Example | End mark |
|---|------|-----|---------|----------|
| 1 | **Assertive / Declarative (Affirmative)** | States a fact | She is a teacher. | . |
| 2 | **Negative** | Denies | She is not a teacher. | . |
| 3 | **Interrogative** | Asks | Is she a teacher? / Where is she? | ? |
| 4 | **Imperative** | Order, request, advice | Open the book. Please sit. | . or ! |
| 5 | **Exclamatory** | Strong feeling | What a bright lab this is! | ! |
| 6 | **Optative** | Wish / prayer | May you succeed! Long live our land! | ! or . |
| 7 | **Conditional** *(or Affirmative as 7th in some lists)* | If-condition | If you work, you will pass. | . |

**Note:** Some keys count **Assertive, Negative, Interrogative, Imperative, Exclamatory, Optative** and then **either Conditional or Affirmative separately**. If the paper says “seven types”, write the table above. Structure types (simple / compound / complex) are a **different** official heading — file 13.

## 3. Transformations (same meaning)

**Assertive → Negative**  
He is honest. → He is not dishonest. / He is not a dishonest man.  
(Use opposite adjective + *not* so meaning stays.)

She always comes late. → She never comes early. / She does not ever come early.

**Assertive → Interrogative**  
He is a teacher. → Is he not a teacher? / Isn’t he a teacher?  
(Meaning: we expect *yes*.)

**Assertive → Exclamatory**  
It is a very beautiful lab. → What a beautiful lab it is! / How beautiful the lab is!

**Exclamatory → Assertive**  
What a fool he is! → He is a great fool.

**Imperative → Assertive**  
Close the door. → You should / must close the door.  
Please help me. → You are requested to help me.

**Optative → Assertive**  
May you live long! → I wish / pray that you may live long.

**Conditional**  
Work hard or you will fail. → If you do not work hard, you will fail.

## 4. MCQs

**1.** “May God bless you!” is:  
a) Imperative  b) Optative  c) Negative  d) Cluster  

**2.** “What a surprise!” is:  
a) Assertive  b) Exclamatory  c) Interrogative  d) Concord  

**3.** “Please open the window” is:  
a) Optative  b) Imperative (request)  c) Only negative  d) Passive only  

**4.** “Does she teach English?” is:  
a) Wh- question  b) Yes/No question  c) Optative  d) Metaphor  

**5.** “If it rains, we shall stay” is:  
a) Only a phrase  b) Conditional  c) Only a noun  d) Onomatopoeia  

**6.** Assertive of “What a tall boy he is!”:  
a) Is he tall?  b) He is a very tall boy.  c) May he be tall.  d) Don’t be tall.  

### Answers

1-b, 2-b, 3-b, 4-b, 5-b, 6-b
