# English 9 — Degrees of Comparison

**Official line:** Sentences: transformations — Degrees of comparison  
**Time:** 45 minutes  

---

## Today's goal

Form **positive, comparative, superlative** and **transform** a sentence from one degree to another **without changing the meaning**.

---

## 1. Three degrees

| Degree | Use | Example |
|--------|-----|---------|
| **Positive** | Quality as it is | Ravi is **tall**. |
| **Comparative** | Compare two | Ravi is **taller than** Kiran. |
| **Superlative** | Among more than two | Ravi is the **tallest** boy in the class. |

---

## 2. How to form

| Type | Comparative | Superlative | Examples |
|------|-------------|-------------|----------|
| Short (1 syllable) | + er | + est | tall–taller–tallest; fast–faster–fastest |
| -y | y → i + er/est | | happy–happier–happiest |
| Long (2+ syllables often) | more / most | | useful–more useful–most useful; beautiful–more–most |
| Irregular | special | | good–**better–best**; bad–**worse–worst**; much/many–**more–most**; little–**less–least**; far–farther/further–farthest/furthest |

*Clever* can be *cleverer* or *more clever*. *Fun* → *more fun* (not *funner* in exams).

**the** before superlative: *the best*.  
**than** after comparative: *taller than*.

---

## 3. Transformations (same meaning)

**Comparative → Superlative**  
Ravi is taller than any other boy in the class.  
→ Ravi is the tallest boy in the class.

**Superlative → Comparative**  
Ravi is the tallest boy in the class.  
→ Ravi is taller than any other boy in the class.

**Positive → Comparative** (using *as…as*)  
Ravi is as tall as Kiran.  
→ Kiran is not taller than Ravi. (or they are equal)

**No other / very few**

- *No other boy is as tall as Ravi.* → Ravi is the tallest.  
- *Very few boys are as tall as Ravi.* → Ravi is one of the tallest boys.

**One of the** + superlative + **plural** noun:  
*one of the best **teachers*** (not *teacher*).

---

## 4. Common errors

- *more better* ✗ → *better*  
- *most unique* (unique is already absolute — papers may mark it wrong)  
- *taller than me* vs *taller than I (am)* — formal exam often likes *than I*  
- Double comparative: *more taller* ✗

---

## 5. MCQs

**1.** Superlative of *good*:  
a) gooder  b) better  c) best  d) more good  

**2.** Comparative of *beautiful*:  
a) beautifuler  b) more beautiful  c) most beautiful  d) beauty  

**3.** “Ravi is the tallest” means:  
a) Someone is taller  b) He is taller than the other boys  c) He is short  d) Equal to all  

**4.** *one of the* takes:  
a) singular noun  b) plural noun  c) no noun  d) only verbs  

**5.** *worse* is the comparative of:  
a) good  b) bad  c) well only  d) far  

### Answers

1-c, 2-b, 3-b, 4-b, 5-b
