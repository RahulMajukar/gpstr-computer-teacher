# General English — Practice Test (Official 6–8 Syllabus)

**Time:** 35 minutes  
**Closed notes.**

---

## A. Grammar and usage

1. *Honesty* is a / an ____ noun.  
2. *She* is a ____.  
3. *Very* in “very kind” is an ____.  
4. Present perfect continuous of *teach* (I): I ____.  
5. After *can* we use the ____ verb.  
6. *You mustn’t* means ____ (prohibition / no need).  
7. “She wrote a letter” — *wrote* is ____ (transitive / intransitive).  
8. “The baby slept” — *slept* is ____.  
9. “in the morning” is an adverbial phrase of ____.  
10. A phrase has ____ finite verb.  
11. Synonym of *begin*: ____.  
12. Antonym of *success*: ____.  
13. Prefix *re-* means ____.  
14. Noun of *decide*: ____.  
15. *First* is an ____ number.  
16. *street* begins with a consonant ____.  
17. /p/ is aspirated in *pin* / *spin*? ____.  
18. Yes/No questions often take ____ intonation.  
19. The quality of the apples ____ good. (is/are)  
20. One of the boys ____ absent. (is/are)  
21. “as brave as a lion” is a ____.  
22. “The sea is a hungry dog” is a ____.  
23. Superlative of *good*: ____.  
24. Comparative of *beautiful*: ____.  
25. Passive of “She writes a letter”: A letter ____ by her.  
26. “May you succeed!” is ____ (type of sentence).  
27. “What a fine day!” is ____.  
28. *Said to* → ____ in indirect speech.  
29. “Are you ready?” → He asked ____ I was ready.  
30. “He came but I left” is a ____ sentence.  
31. “If you try, you will win” is ____.  
32. “that she is honest” after *I know* is a ____ clause.  
33. “who taught us” after *the teacher* is an ____ clause.  
34. “because he was late” is an adverb clause of ____.  
35. A main clause ____ stand alone.

---

## B. Mini comprehension

*The old banyan in the school yard had seen a hundred monsoons. Children still climbed its roots. The HM said it was not just a tree but a teacher of patience.*

36. Local/factual: What tree is named?  
37. Inferential: Why is the tree called a teacher?  
38. “a hundred monsoons” is mainly ____ (hyperbole / cluster).  
39. Global: The passage is about ____.  
40. “not just a tree but a teacher” is closest to a ____ (simile / metaphor).
