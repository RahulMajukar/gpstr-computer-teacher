## 1. What a verb does

A **verb** shows **action** (*teach*) or **state** (*is, seem, have*).

| Kind | Example |
|------|---------|
| **Main / principal** | write, go, teach |
| **Auxiliary / helping** | be, have, do |
| **Modal** | can, may, must, should… |

*She **is writing**.* → *is* = auxiliary; *writing* = main (present participle).

---

## 2. Time vs tense vs aspect

- **Time** = when (past / present / future) — real world.  
- **Tense** = the **verb form** we use.  
- **Aspect** = how the action is viewed: **simple**, **continuous (progressive)**, **perfect**, **perfect continuous**.

**3 times × 4 aspects = 12 forms** (exam table).

---

## 3. The 12 forms of *teach* / *write* (learn one verb fully)

### Present

| Aspect | Form | Use |
|--------|------|-----|
| Simple | I teach / she **teaches** | Habit, fact |
| Continuous | I **am teaching** | Now / around now |
| Perfect | I **have taught** | Past with link to now |
| Perfect continuous | I **have been teaching** | Started in past, still going |

### Past

| Aspect | Form | Use |
|--------|------|-----|
| Simple | I **taught** | Finished past time |
| Continuous | I **was teaching** | In progress at a past moment |
| Perfect | I **had taught** | Before another past action |
| Perfect continuous | I **had been teaching** | Duration before a past point |

### Future

| Aspect | Form | Use |
|--------|------|-----|
| Simple | I **will teach** | Later |
| Continuous | I **will be teaching** | In progress in the future |
| Perfect | I **will have taught** | Finished before a future time |
| Perfect continuous | I **will have been teaching** | Duration up to a future time |

**Signal words**

- Simple present: *every day, always, usually*  
- Present continuous: *now, at the moment*  
- Present perfect: *already, yet, just, since, for*  
- Simple past: *yesterday, last year, in 2020*  
- Past perfect: *before, after, already* (two past times)  
- Future: *tomorrow, next week*

**since** + point (*since 2020*). **for** + period (*for two years*).

## 4. Auxiliaries: be, have, do

| Auxiliary | Forms | Job |
|-----------|-------|-----|
| **Be** | am, is, are, was, were, being, been | Continuous (*is writing*); passive (*is written*) |
| **Have** | have, has, had | Perfect (*has written*) |
| **Do** | do, does, did | Questions / negatives in simple tenses (*Does she teach? She did not go.*) |

**Subject–verb:** *He **does** / **has** / **is***. *They **do** / **have** / **are***.

---

## 5. Modals

Modals do **not** take *-s* in the third person. After a modal, use the **base verb** (*She can **go***, not *goes*).

| Modal | Main meaning | Example |
|-------|--------------|---------|
| **can / could** | ability; polite request; possibility | She can type. Could you help? |
| **may / might** | permission; possibility | You may go. It might rain. |
| **must** | strong necessity / deduction | You must wear the ID. He must be tired. |
| **have to** | external necessity | We have to finish the syllabus. |
| **should / ought to** | advice / duty | You should revise daily. |
| **shall** | offer / formal future (I/we) | Shall I open the lab? |
| **will / would** | future; habit; polite | Would you sit down? |
| **need** | necessity (*need not* = no need) | You need not come. |
| **dare** | courage (rare) | How dare you! |
| **used to** | past habit | He used to walk to school. |

**Mustn’t** = prohibition. **Needn’t** = not necessary. Different!

*You mustn’t copy.* (do not)  
*You needn’t copy the whole page.* (not required)

---

## 6. Finite vs non-finite (short)

- **Finite:** shows tense and subject — *She **writes***.  
- **Non-finite:** infinitive *to write*, gerund *writing*, participle *written / writing*.

## 7. MCQs

**1.** *She has been teaching since 2018* is:  
a) Simple past  b) Present perfect continuous  c) Future perfect  d) Simple present  

**2.** After a modal we use:  
a) -s form  b) Base verb  c) -ing always  d) Past participle always  

**3.** *Does* is used with:  
a) I / you / we  b) He / she / it  c) They only  d) Past tense  

**4.** *You mustn’t* means:  
a) No need  b) Prohibition  c) Ability  d) Past habit  

**5.** *used to* shows:  
a) Future  b) Past habit  c) Permission now  d) Passive only  

**6.** *I had left before he came* uses:  
a) Past perfect + simple past  b) Two futures  c) Only present  d) Modal must  

**7.** Continuous aspect uses:  
a) have + V3  b) be + V-ing  c) do + V1  d) modal + -s  

**8.** *since* is used with:  
a) a period only  b) a point of time  c) only future  d) only adjectives  

### Answers

1-b, 2-b, 3-b, 4-b, 5-b, 6-a, 7-b, 8-b
