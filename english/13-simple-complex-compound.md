# English 13 — Simple, Complex, and Compound Sentences

**Official line:** Simple, complex and compound sentences  
**Time:** 50 minutes  

---

## Today's goal

Count **finite verbs** and **clauses**. Transform simple ↔ compound ↔ complex.

---

## 1. Definitions

| Type | Structure | Example |
|------|-----------|---------|
| **Simple** | **One** finite verb (one independent clause). May have phrases. | The tired teacher sat down. |
| **Compound** | **Two or more independent** clauses joined by *and, but, or, so, yet, for, nor* (coordinating). | She taught and the class listened. |
| **Complex** | **One main** + **one or more subordinate** clauses (*because, when, if, who, that…*). | She sat down **because she was tired**. |

**Finite verb** = a verb that shows tense (*sat, taught, was*).  
*Sitting on the chair, she smiled.* = **simple** (only *smiled* is finite; *sitting* is a participle).

---

## 2. Coordinating conjunctions (FANBOYS)

**For, And, Nor, But, Or, Yet, So** — make **compound** sentences.

*He was ill, **so** he stayed at home.*

---

## 3. Subordinating conjunctions (complex)

*because, as, since, if, unless, when, while, before, after, although, though, that, so that, who, which, whom, whose, where…*

*Although he was ill, he came to school.* (complex)

---

## 4. Transformations

**Simple → Compound**  
Being tired, she sat. → She was tired **and** she sat.

**Simple → Complex**  
Being tired, she sat. → She sat **because** she was tired.

**Compound → Complex**  
Work hard **or** you will fail. → **If** you do not work hard, you will fail.

**Complex → Simple**  
She sat because she was tired. → Being tired, she sat. / She sat due to tiredness.

**Complex → Compound**  
She sat because she was tired. → She was tired, so she sat.

---

## 5. Spotting drill

1. *The sun rose.* — simple  
2. *The sun rose and the birds sang.* — compound  
3. *When the sun rose, the birds sang.* — complex  
4. *The girl who won the prize is my student.* — complex (relative clause)  
5. *In spite of the rain they played.* — simple (phrase, not clause)

---

## 6. MCQs

**1.** A simple sentence has:  
a) Two finite verbs always  b) One finite verb  c) No verb  d) Only modals  

**2.** “He came but I left” is:  
a) Simple  b) Compound  c) Only a phrase  d) Passive  

**3.** “If you try, you will win” is:  
a) Compound  b) Complex  c) Simple  d) Optative only  

**4.** FANBOYS join:  
a) Subordinate clauses only  b) Independent clauses (compound)  c) Only nouns  d) Only prefixes  

**5.** “The boy standing there is Ravi” is:  
a) Complex  b) Simple  c) Compound  d) A cluster  

### Answers

1-b, 2-b, 3-b, 4-b, 5-b

---

## Quick revision

Simple = 1 finite verb. Compound = and/but/or… Complex = because/when/who…. Participle phrases keep it simple.
