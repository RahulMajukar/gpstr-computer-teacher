# English 12 — Reported Speech (Direct and Indirect)

**Official line:** Reported speech  
**Time:** 60 minutes  

---

## Today's goal

Change **direct** (“…”) to **indirect** with the right **tense, pronoun, time word, and conjunction**.

---

## 1. Definitions

> **Direct speech:** the speaker’s exact words in quotation marks.  
> **Indirect / reported speech:** we report the meaning, no quotes.

Direct: He said, “I am tired.”  
Indirect: He said that he **was** tired.

**Reporting verb:** *said, told, asked, requested, ordered, exclaimed, advised.*  
*Said to* → **told** + object: *He told me that…*

---

## 2. Tense change (if reporting verb is **past**)

| Direct | Indirect |
|--------|----------|
| Simple present | Simple past |
| Present continuous | Past continuous |
| Present perfect | Past perfect |
| Simple past | Past perfect |
| Past continuous | Past perfect continuous |
| will / can / may | would / could / might |
| must (necessity) | had to / must |
| shall | should / would |

**No change** if the reporting verb is **present/future**:  
She **says**, “I am busy.” → She says that she **is** busy.

**No change** for universal truth:  
He said, “The sun **rises** in the east.” → He said that the sun **rises** in the east.

## 3. Pronouns and words of nearness

| Direct | Indirect |
|--------|----------|
| I / we | he-she / they (as sense needs) |
| my / our | his-her / their |
| this / these | that / those |
| here | there |
| now | then |
| today | that day |
| tomorrow | the next day / the following day |
| yesterday | the previous day / the day before |
| tonight | that night |
| last week | the previous week |
| next week | the following week |
| ago | before |
| come | go (sometimes) |

## 4. Sentence types

### Statement

*that* + tense change.  
He said, “I like tea.” → He said that he liked tea.

### Yes/No question

*if / whether*. Question order becomes **statement** order.  
He said, “Are you ready?” → He asked **if I was** ready.

### Wh- question

Keep the Wh-word. No *do/does/did* helper.  
He said, “Where do you live?” → He asked **where I lived**.

### Imperative

*told / ordered / requested / advised* + **to / not to** + verb.  
“Open the book,” she said. → She **told me to** open the book.  
“Don’t run,” he said. → He **told us not to** run.  
“Please help me.” → He **requested me to** help him.

### Exclamatory / optative

*exclaimed that / wished that / prayed that*.  
He said, “What a fine day!” → He exclaimed that it was a very fine day.  
She said, “May you win!” → She wished / prayed that I might win.

## 5. MCQs

**1.** *Said to* in indirect becomes:  
a) say  b) told  c) ask always  d) must  

**2.** “I am late,” he said. → He said that he ____ late.  
a) is  b) was  c) will  d) been  

**3.** Yes/No questions use:  
a) that only  b) if / whether  c) to  d) than  

**4.** “Where is the lab?” she said. → She asked where the lab ____.  
a) is  b) was  c) were  d) be  

**5.** “Don’t shout,” the teacher said. → The teacher told us ____ shout.  
a) to  b) not to  c) that  d) if  

**6.** Universal truth: tense usually:  
a) Always becomes past  b) Does not change  c) Becomes future  d) Becomes modal  

### Answers

1-b, 2-b, 3-b, 4-b, 5-b, 6-b
