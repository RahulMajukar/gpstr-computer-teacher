# English 7 — Syntax and Concord (Agreement)

**Official line:** Syntax: subject–verb–object agreement (syntax concord)  
**Time:** 55 minutes  

---

## Today's goal

Make the **verb** agree with the **subject** (not with a nearby noun). Know SVO order.

---

## 1. Syntax in one line

> **Syntax** is the order and relation of words in a sentence.

Basic English order: **SVO** — Subject + Verb + Object  
*The teacher (S) taught (V) grammar (O).*

Questions invert: *Did the teacher teach grammar?*  
Passives move the object: *Grammar was taught.*

---

## 2. Concord = agreement

> The verb must match the subject in **number** and **person**.

| Subject | Verb |
|---------|------|
| I | am / was / have / do / write |
| He / She / It / Ravi / the class (as one) | is / was / has / does / writes |
| We / You / They / the students | are / were / have / do / write |

*The **quality** of the apples **is** good.* (quality = singular)  
Not: *are* (do not agree with *apples*).

---

## 3. Rules the paper loves

| Rule | Example |
|------|---------|
| Two subjects with **and** → plural | Ravi and Asha **are** here. |
| **Each / every / everyone / nobody** → singular | Everyone **is** ready. |
| **Either…or / neither…nor** → verb follows the **nearer** subject | Neither the teachers nor the HM **is** late. / Neither the HM nor the teachers **are** late. |
| **Collective noun** as one unit → singular | The team **has** won. |
| Collective as members → plural (esp. British) | The team **are** wearing new jerseys. |
| **A number of** → plural | A number of books **are** missing. |
| **The number of** → singular | The number of books **is** ten. |
| Units of money / time / distance → singular | Ten years **is** a long time. Five hundred rupees **is** enough. |
| **There is / are** → agree with the real subject after | There **are** two labs. There **is** a lab. |
| Relative *who/which/that* → agree with the **antecedent** | I who **am** your teacher… / He who **is**… |
| **One of the** + plural noun + singular verb | One of the boys **is** absent. |
| **With / as well as / along with** → verb follows the **first** subject | The HM, with the teachers, **is** coming. |
| Uncountable → singular | The news **is** good. Mathematics **is** useful. |
| **Police / people / cattle** → plural | The police **have** arrived. |
| **Either / neither** as subject → singular | Neither **is** correct. |

---

## 4. Pronoun concord

*Each student must bring **his or her** book.* (or plural *students… their* in modern use)  
*One should do **one’s** duty.*

Object form: *between you and **me*** (not I).

---

## 5. Error-spotting method

1. Find the **real subject** (ignore phrases after *of, with, as well as*).  
2. Decide singular / plural.  
3. Check the verb.  
4. Check pronoun.

## 6. MCQs

**1.** The quality of the mangoes ____ good.  
a) are  b) is  c) were  d) have  

**2.** Neither of the answers ____ correct.  
a) are  b) is  c) have  d) were  

**3.** A number of students ____ absent.  
a) is  b) are  c) has  d) was  

**4.** The number of students ____ fifty.  
a) are  b) is  c) have  d) were  

**5.** The HM, with the clerks, ____ arrived.  
a) have  b) has  c) are  d) were  

**6.** One of the girls ____ won.  
a) have  b) has  c) are  d) were  

**7.** Ten kilometres ____ a long walk.  
a) are  b) is  c) were  d) have  

**8.** There ____ two computers in the lab.  
a) is  b) are  c) was  d) has  

### Answers

1-b, 2-b, 3-b, 4-b, 5-b, 6-b, 7-b, 8-b

---

## Quick revision

SVO. Verb follows the **head subject**. *and* → plural. *either/or* → nearer. *one of* → singular. *a number of* plural; *the number of* singular.
