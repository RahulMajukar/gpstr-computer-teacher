# English 6 — Pronunciation

**Official line:** cluster, aspirated sounds, stress and intonation  
**Time:** 50 minutes  
**Do this file aloud.**

---

## Today's goal

1. What a consonant **cluster** is  
2. What **aspiration** is (p, t, k)  
3. Word stress and sentence stress  
4. Rising vs falling **intonation**

---

## 1. Sounds, not letters

English has about **44 sounds** (phonemes): vowels + consonants.  
Spelling is not a safe guide (*put / but*, *though / through*).

**IPA** (names only): /iː/ *see*, /æ/ *cat*, /θ/ *think*, /ð/ *this*.

---

## 2. Consonant clusters

> A **cluster** is two or more consonant **sounds** together in one syllable, with no vowel between them.

| Place | Examples |
|-------|----------|
| Beginning | *st*ar, *str*eet, *spl*ash, *pl*ay, *cr*y, *tw*in |
| End | a*sk*, te*xt*, la*mps*, *nth* (strength) |
| Three consonants | *str*ong, *spr*ing, *scr*een |

**Kannada speakers — common error:** adding an extra vowel  
*school* → not “i-school”; *street* → not “si-treet”.

**Consonant + /j/**: *tune, student, news* (in many accents /tj/, /stj/, /nj/).

**Minimal pair practice:** *play / pay*, *cry / guy*, *slip / sip*.

---

## 3. Aspirated sounds

> **Aspiration** is a small puff of air after some consonants.

In English, **voiceless stops /p, t, k/** are **aspirated** at the **start of a stressed syllable**:

| Sound | Aspirated | Less / not aspirated |
|-------|-----------|----------------------|
| /p/ | **p**in, **p**aper | s**p**in (after /s/) |
| /t/ | **t**ime, **t**each | s**t**and |
| /k/ | **k**ing, **c**ome | s**k**ill |

Hold a paper in front of your mouth: *pin* moves it more than *spin*.

**H-sound** /h/ in *hat, house* is a separate fricative; do not drop it (*hat* ≠ *at*).

Indian languages also have aspirated letters (ಫ, ಥ, ಖ). English aspiration is **weaker** and follows the /p t k/ rule above.

## 4. Stress

### Word stress

One syllable is **stronger**. Wrong stress can change the word.

| Noun / adjective (often first) | Verb (often second) |
|--------------------------------|---------------------|
| **RE**-cord | re-**CORD** |
| **PRE**-sent | pre-**SENT** |
| **OB**-ject | ob-**JECT** |
| **CON**-duct | con-**DUCT** |

Other: **TEacher**, com**PUter**, under**STAND**, **INternet**.

**Schwa /ə/** (weak vowel) appears in unstressed syllables: *a*bout, teach*er*.

### Sentence stress

Content words (nouns, main verbs, adjectives, adverbs) are stressed.  
Grammar words (*a, the, of, to, is*) are usually weak.

*I want to **TEACH** in a **SCHOOL**.*

**Contrastive stress:** *I* want tea (not he). I want **TEA** (not coffee).

---

## 5. Intonation (tune of the sentence)

| Tune | Shape | Use |
|------|-------|-----|
| **Falling** ↘ | Voice goes down | Statements, *Wh-* questions, commands | *She is a teacher.* *Where do you live?* *Sit down.* |
| **Rising** ↗ | Voice goes up | Yes/No questions, doubt, politeness | *Are you ready?* *She’s coming?* |
| **Fall-rise** | Down then up | Reservation, “but…” | *I like it…* |

**Yes/No:** *Is the lab open?* ↗  
**Wh-:** *When is the lab open?* ↘  

---

## 6. Teaching tip (Class 6–8)

Clap the stressed syllable. Use a paper slip for aspiration. Do not shame accent; teach **clarity**.

## 7. MCQs

**1.** A consonant cluster is:  
a) Two vowels  b) Two or more consonants together  c) Only stress  d) A tense  

**2.** /p/ is aspirated in:  
a) spin  b) pin  c) only after s  d) never  

**3.** *REcord* (noun) vs *reCORD* (verb) shows:  
a) Intonation only  b) Word stress shift  c) A cluster of 5  d) Passive  

**4.** Yes/No questions usually take:  
a) Falling  b) Rising intonation  c) No tune  d) Only stress on *the*  

**5.** *street* begins with:  
a) One consonant  b) A cluster /str/  c) A modal  d) An article  

**6.** Unstressed vowel is often:  
a) Schwa /ə/  b) Always /iː/  c) Silent  d) A prefix  

### Answers

1-b, 2-b, 3-b, 4-b, 5-b, 6-a

---

## Quick revision

Cluster = consonants together (*str, spl*). Aspirate p/t/k at start (*pin* not *spin*). Stress changes *REcord/reCORD*. Falling = statement/Wh; rising = Yes/No.
