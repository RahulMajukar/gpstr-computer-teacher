# English 3 — Transitive and Intransitive Verbs

**Official line:** Verbs: transitive and intransitives  
**Time:** 40 minutes  

---

## Today's goal

Ask **“what?”** or **“whom?”** after the verb. If there is an answer, it is **transitive**.

---

## 1. Definitions

> A **transitive verb** needs an **object** to complete the meaning.  
> An **intransitive verb** does **not** take an object.

| | Sentence | Object? |
|--|----------|---------|
| Transitive | The teacher **taught** *grammar*. | grammar |
| Intransitive | The child **slept**. | none |
| Intransitive | Birds **fly**. | none |

Test: *Taught what?* → grammar. *Slept what?* → no answer.

---

## 2. Direct and indirect object

Some transitive verbs take **two** objects.

*She gave **the students** **a book**.*

- **Indirect object** (to whom): the students  
- **Direct object** (what): a book  

Also: *She gave a book **to the students**.*

---

## 3. Same verb, both uses (exam favourite)

| Verb | Transitive | Intransitive |
|------|------------|--------------|
| run | He **runs** a school. | He **runs** fast. |
| grow | They **grow** rice. | The child **grows**. |
| sing | She **sang** a song. | She **sang** well. |
| write | He **wrote** a letter. | He **writes** neatly. |
| open | Open the door. | The shop **opens** at 9. |

---

## 4. Linking verbs (not really “action + object”)

*Be, seem, become, look, feel* join the subject to a **complement**, not an object.

*She **is** a teacher.* (*teacher* = complement, not a thing she “does to”)

Do not call this a normal transitive object.

---

## 5. Why it matters

Only **transitive** verbs can normally become **passive**:

*The teacher taught grammar.* → *Grammar was taught (by the teacher).*  
*The child slept.* → **no** passive.

---

## 6. MCQs

**1.** In “She wrote a poem”, *wrote* is:  
a) Intransitive  b) Transitive  c) Modal  d) Article  

**2.** “The baby cried” — *cried* is:  
a) Transitive  b) Intransitive  c) Preposition  d) Noun  

**3.** Which can be made passive?  
a) He arrived.  b) They built a lab.  c) She slept.  d) It rained.  

**4.** In “Give me the chalk”, *me* is:  
a) Direct object  b) Indirect object  c) Subject  d) Adverb  

**5.** “He runs a shop” — *runs* is:  
a) Intransitive  b) Transitive  c) Interjection  d) Concord error  

### Answers

1-b, 2-b, 3-b, 4-b, 5-b

---

## Quick revision

Transitive = object. Intransitive = no object. Same verb can be both. Passive needs a transitive verb.
