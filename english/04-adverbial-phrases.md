# English 4 — Adverbial Phrases

**Official line:** Adverbial phrases  
**Time:** 40 minutes  

---

## Today's goal

Spot a group of words that does the work of an **adverb** (how, when, where, why).

---

## 1. Phrase vs clause (one line)

- **Phrase:** no finite verb — *in the morning*  
- **Clause:** has a subject + finite verb — *when the bell rang*

An **adverbial phrase** is a phrase that modifies a verb (or the whole sentence) like an adverb.

---

## 2. Types (by question)

| Kind | Question | Examples |
|------|----------|----------|
| **Manner** | How? | *with care, in a hurry, as fast as possible* |
| **Time** | When? | *in the morning, after school, at once* |
| **Place** | Where? | *in the lab, near the gate, at home* |
| **Frequency** | How often? | *every day, once a week* |
| **Purpose** | Why? (to…) | *to pass the exam, for extra marks* |
| **Reason** | Why? | *because of the rain* (phrase) |
| **Degree** | How much? | *to a great extent* |

*She spoke **in a low voice**.* (manner)  
*We met **after the assembly**.* (time)  
*Sit **near the window**.* (place)

---

## 3. Adverb vs adverbial phrase

| Adverb (one word) | Adverbial phrase |
|-------------------|------------------|
| quickly | in a quick manner / at once |
| yesterday | on Monday morning |
| here | in this classroom |

---

## 4. Prepositional phrases as adverbials

Many adverbial phrases start with a **preposition**: *in, on, at, after, before, with, without, by*.

*He finished the work **in two hours**.*  
*Do not write **without a ruler**.*

If the same prepositional phrase describes a **noun**, it is **adjectival**:  
*The book **on the table** is mine.* (describes *book*)

---

## 5. MCQs

**1.** “She sang in a sweet voice” — the italic idea is:  
a) Noun clause  b) Adverbial phrase of manner  c) Adjective only  d) Modal  

**2.** “We play in the evening” — *in the evening* is:  
a) Time phrase  b) Manner  c) Subject  d) Object  

**3.** A phrase has:  
a) A finite verb  b) No finite verb  c) Always a modal  d) Only nouns  

**4.** “The girl in the lab is Rani” — *in the lab* here is mainly:  
a) Adverbial of time  b) Adjectival (describes girl)  c) Interjection  d) Concord  

**5.** “He came to see the HM” — *to see the HM* shows:  
a) Place  b) Purpose  c) Number  d) Gender  

### Answers

1-b, 2-a, 3-b, 4-b, 5-b

---

## Quick revision

Adverbial phrase = group of words acting as an adverb. How / when / where / why. Phrase ≠ clause.
