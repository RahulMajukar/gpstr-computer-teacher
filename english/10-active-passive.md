# English 10 — Active and Passive Voice

**Official line:** Active and passive voice  
**Time:** 50 minutes  

---

## Today's goal

Change voice **keeping the same tense**. Only **transitive** verbs have a full passive.

---

## 1. Definitions

> **Active:** the subject **does** the action.  
> **Passive:** the subject **receives** the action.

Active: *The teacher **teaches** the class.*  
Passive: *The class **is taught** by the teacher.*

Formula: **object → subject** + **be (same tense)** + **past participle (V3)** + *(by + doer)*

---

## 2. Tense chart (memorise)

| Tense | Active | Passive |
|-------|--------|---------|
| Simple present | writes | **is/are written** |
| Present continuous | is writing | **is/are being written** |
| Present perfect | has written | **has/have been written** |
| Simple past | wrote | **was/were written** |
| Past continuous | was writing | **was/were being written** |
| Past perfect | had written | **had been written** |
| Simple future | will write | **will be written** |
| Future perfect | will have written | **will have been written** |
| Modal | can write | **can be written** |

**No common passive** for present/past perfect continuous and future continuous in school exams (awkward). Skip them.

---

## 3. Extra patterns

**Two objects**  
*She gave me a book.*  
→ *I was given a book (by her).*  
→ *A book was given to me (by her).*

**Questions**  
*Did she write the letter?* → *Was the letter written by her?*  
*Who wrote it?* → *By whom was it written?* / *Who was it written by?*

**Imperative**  
*Open the door.* → *Let the door be opened.*  
*Please help me.* → *You are requested to help me.*

**have to**  
*We have to finish it.* → *It has to be finished.*

**Don’t mention *by*** when the doer is obvious or unknown:  
*English is spoken in India.*

---

## 4. When not to use passive

Intransitive: *He arrived.* (no object)  
Unless a dummy: *People say…* → *It is said that…*

## 5. MCQs

**1.** Passive of “She writes a letter”:  
a) A letter wrote she  b) A letter is written by her  c) A letter was write  d) She is written a letter  

**2.** “The lab was cleaned” is:  
a) Active  b) Passive  c) Modal only  d) A cluster  

**3.** V3 means:  
a) Base verb  b) Past participle  c) -ing  d) Modal  

**4.** Passive of “Can they solve it?”:  
a) Can it be solved by them?  b) Can it solved?  c) It can solve them  d) They are solved  

**5.** “Let the door be opened” comes from:  
a) A question  b) An imperative  c) Past perfect  d) A simile  

### Answers

1-b, 2-b, 3-b, 4-a, 5-b
