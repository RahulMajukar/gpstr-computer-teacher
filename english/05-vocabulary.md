## 5. Derivations (word family)

One **root**, many forms:

| Root | Noun | Verb | Adjective | Adverb |
|------|------|------|-----------|--------|
| kind | kindness | — | kind | kindly |
| beauty | beauty | beautify | beautiful | beautifully |
| success | success | succeed | successful | successfully |
| strong | strength | strengthen | strong | strongly |
| nation | nation / nationality | nationalise | national | nationally |
| educate | education | educate | educational | educationally |

## 6. Numbers (syllabus word)

### Cardinal vs ordinal

| Cardinal (how many) | Ordinal (position) |
|---------------------|--------------------|
| one, two, three | first, second, third |
| four, five | fourth, fifth |
| nine, twelve | ninth, twelfth |
| twenty-one | twenty-first |
| hundred | hundredth |

*Class **six*** (cardinal). *He stood **first*** (ordinal).

### Other number words

| Idea | Words |
|------|-------|
| Pair / two | pair, couple, both, twice, double |
| Three | triple, thrice (old), trio |
| Many | dozen (12), score (20) |
| Fractions | half, quarter, one-third |
| Collective | a herd, a class, a fleet |

**Subject–verb:** *Two and two **make** four.* *A hundred rupees **is** enough* (sum as one). Papers vary — learn both patterns if options appear.

**Hyphen:** *a five-year-old child* (adjective). *The child is five years old.*

**6.** *Impossible* uses prefix:
