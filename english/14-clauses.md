# English 14 — Clauses (Identifying Types)

**Official line:** Clauses: identifying the types of clauses  
**Time:** 50 minutes  

---

## Today's goal

Find every clause. Label **main** vs **subordinate**. Then label subordinate as **noun / adjective / adverb**.

---

## 1. What is a clause?

> A **clause** is a group of words with a **subject** and a **finite verb**.

*when the bell rang* — subject *bell*, verb *rang* → clause.  
*in the morning* — no finite verb → **phrase**, not a clause.

---

## 2. Main vs subordinate

| Kind | Job | Example |
|------|-----|---------|
| **Main / independent / principal** | Can stand alone | **She smiled** |
| **Subordinate / dependent** | Cannot stand alone; needs a main clause | **when the bell rang** |

*She smiled when the bell rang.*  
Main: she smiled. Subordinate: when the bell rang.

---

## 3. Three subordinate types

### A. Noun clause (does the work of a noun)

Answers *what?* Can be subject, object, or complement.

- *I know **that he is honest**.* (object of *know*)  
- ***What she said** is true.* (subject)  
- *The news is **that the exam is near**.* (complement)  
- *I do not know **where he lives**.*

Starters: *that, what, whatever, who, whether, if, why, when, where, how*.

### B. Adjective / relative clause (describes a noun)

Comes after a noun. Starters: *who, whom, whose, which, that, where* (after a place noun).

- *The teacher **who taught us** has retired.*  
- *This is the lab **that we use**.*  
- *The town **where I was born** is small.*

**Defining** (needed for meaning, often no commas) vs **non-defining** (extra, commas):  
*My brother who lives in Mysuru is a teacher.* (which brother)  
*My brother, who lives in Mysuru, is a teacher.* (only one brother; extra fact)

### C. Adverb clause (does the work of an adverb)

Tells time, place, reason, condition, concession, purpose, comparison, manner.

| Kind | Linker | Example |
|------|--------|---------|
| Time | when, while, before, after, till, as soon as | *When it rains, we stay in.* |
| Place | where, wherever | *Sit where you like.* |
| Reason | because, as, since | *As he was ill, he rested.* |
| Condition | if, unless, provided | *If you try, you will pass.* |
| Concession | although, though, even if | *Though he is poor, he is honest.* |
| Purpose | so that, in order that | *He works so that he may pass.* |
| Result | so…that | *He is so weak that he cannot walk.* |
| Comparison | than, as…as | *She is taller than I am.* |
| Manner | as, as if | *Do as I do.* |

---

## 4. Identification method

1. Find finite verbs.  
2. Split into clauses.  
3. Which clause can stand alone? → main.  
4. What job does the other do? noun / adjective / adverb.  
5. For adverb, name the kind (time, reason…).

## 5. MCQs

**1.** “when the sun rose” is:  
a) A phrase  b) A clause  c) A prefix  d) A modal  

**2.** In “I know that she is right”, *that she is right* is:  
a) Adjective clause  b) Noun clause  c) Adverb clause of place  d) Interjection  

**3.** “The book which I bought is new” — *which I bought* is:  
a) Noun clause  b) Adjective clause  c) Adverb of manner  d) Phrase  

**4.** “If you work, you will pass” — *If you work* is:  
a) Noun clause  b) Adverb clause of condition  c) Adjective clause  d) Main only  

**5.** A main clause:  
a) Cannot stand alone  b) Can stand alone  c) Has no verb  d) Is always a simile  

**6.** “because he was late” is:  
a) Adverb clause of reason  b) Noun clause  c) Only a word  d) Concord  

### Answers

1-b, 2-b, 3-b, 4-b, 5-b, 6-a
