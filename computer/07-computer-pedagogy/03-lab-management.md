# Chapter PED-3 — Practical Computer Lab Management

**Paper:** Paper-II Computer Pedagogy

---

## Today's goal

1. Lab layout and safety  
2. Rules poster  
3. Timetable, log book, stock  
4. Few PCs — how to teach  
5. 5-mark “how will you manage a school lab?”  

---

## 1. Why labs fail

Dust, no UPS, shared passwords, games, missing mice, no seating plan, teacher talking for 40 minutes. Your answers must sound like you have **run a lab**.

---

## 2. Physical setup

- **Star LAN**, switch in a ventilated rack  
- **UPS / stabilizer**; earthing  
- Projector + whiteboard  
- Teacher PC facing students  
- Cables tied; no loose power strips on the floor  
- Fire extinguisher (CO2 / as per school) — know the location  
- Curtains for projector; no direct sun on screens  
- 2 students per PC if machines are few (written pairing chart)  
- Wheelchair space if needed  

---

## 3. Lab rules (put on the wall)

1. Bags in the rack; shoes if school policy  
2. Login with own id; lock when you leave (Win+L)  
3. No food, no pendrive from home without scan  
4. Do not change wallpaper / settings  
5. Save to **your folder** only  
6. Raise hand; do not bang the keyboard  
7. Shutdown sequence at the bell  
8. Report damage immediately  

---

## 4. Registers a CST must keep

| Register | Why |
|----------|-----|
| Stock (CPU, monitor, mouse, key numbers) | Audit |
| Issue / movement | Printer, projector |
| Student attendance in lab | Period record |
| Fault / complaint | Repair follow-up |
| Software licence list | Legal |
| Lesson / practical record | Inspection |

**AMC** — Annual Maintenance Contract (if any). Keep vendor numbers.

---

## 5. Software discipline

- Licensed Windows/Office **or** legal open source (Ubuntu + LibreOffice)  
- Nudi installed on teacher + student machines  
- Antivirus updated  
- BIOS/user passwords with HM, not on a sticky note on the monitor  
- Deep Freeze / restore point idea (name) to undo student mess  

---

## 6. Teaching when you have 10 PCs and 40 children

- **Rotation:** Group A lab, Group B flowchart on paper, swap  
- **Demo station** + 4 watchers, then rotate  
- **Peer tutors** (trained Class 8)  
- Never leave the lab unattended  

---

## 7. Safety and ethics

- Electrical: dry hands, do not open SMPS  
- Cyber: filter, no YouTube chaos, teacher walks  
- Health: posture, 20-20-20 eyes, time limit  
- Green: shutdown, recycle e-waste via authorised centre  

---

## 8. 5-mark answer

**Question:** As a computer teacher, how will you manage the school computer lab?

Write: layout + UPS; rules; pairing; stock and fault registers; licensed software + Nudi; rotation if PCs are few; cyber and electrical safety; shutdown ritual. End with “lab is a classroom, not a game room.”

---

## Short Qs

1. Two students per PC needs: a) no plan b) pairing chart c) only lecture d) format C  
2. Pendrives from home: a) always free b) scan / restrict c) plug into server blindly d) hide from teacher  
3. Deep Freeze helps: a) cooling coffee b) restore lab image c) increase RAM chips d) draw ER  

**Answers:** 1-b, 2-b, 3-b
