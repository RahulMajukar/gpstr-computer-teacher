## 1. Why this chapter matters

You will teach **Classes 1–8**. Children share photos, click ads, and may be bullied online. A CST is also a **safety teacher**.

---

## 2. Ten lab / home rules (memorise)

1. Do not share passwords, OTP, Aadhaar, or address.  
2. Tell a teacher/parent if a stranger chats.  
3. Do not click unknown links or pop-ups.  
4. Use school accounts only for learning.  
5. No downloading games on lab PCs.  
6. Check **https** and the padlock.  
7. Think before posting a photo of classmates.  
8. Do not mock anyone on groups (cyberbullying).  
9. Time limit: screens are not 24 hours.  
10. Report, do not reply, to abuse — save evidence (screenshot) for the HM.

---

## 3. Netiquette

Polite language, no ALL CAPS shouting, credit others’ work (no copy-paste as your own), do not spam the class group.

---

## 4. Cyberbullying

Repeated harm using phones or internet: insults, fake IDs, excluding, sharing images.

**Teacher response:** listen, do not blame the child, inform HM/parents, preserve evidence, involve counsellor if needed, follow school SOP. Never forward the abusive content.

---

## 5. Legal one-liners (do not quote sections unless sure)

- **IT Act 2000** (amended 2008) — cyber offences in India  
- **POCSO** — sexual offences against children (online too)  
- School must follow **child protection** guidelines  

For the exam: “Cyber crime is punishable under the IT Act; children are extra protected.”

---

## 6. Digital citizenship (pedagogy word)

A good digital citizen is **safe, respectful, and responsible** online — same values as offline (Paper-I value education link).
