## 2. Indian polity (high frequency)

- **Constitution:** 26 Jan 1950; drafted by Dr B.R. Ambedkar (chairman, drafting committee)  
- **Preamble:** Sovereign, Socialist, Secular, Democratic, Republic  
- **Fundamental Rights:** Arts 12–35 (equality, freedom, religion, culture, constitutional remedies — Art 32)  
- **DPSP:** welfare goals, not enforceable like FR  
- **Fundamental Duties:** Art 51A  
- **Parliament:** LS + RS + President  
- **President, PM, CJI, Governor, CM** — know current names from news  
- **Supreme Court, High Courts**  
- **Election Commission, CAG, UPSC**  
- **RTE Act 2009** — education 6–14  
- **NITI Aayog** replaced Planning Commission

## 3. Karnataka — must list

### General

- State formed: **1 November 1956** (Rajyotsava); name Karnataka from **1973** (was Mysore State)  
- Capital: **Bengaluru**; High Court: **Bengaluru** (bench also)  
- Official language: **Kannada**  
- Legislature: bicameral (Vidhan Sabha + Vidhan Parishad)  
- State animal: **Elephant**; bird: **Indian roller (Neelakantha)**; flower: **Lotus**; tree: **Sandal**  
- Dance: **Yakshagana**; music: Hindustani + Carnatic  
- Script: Kannada  

### History (names for MCQ)

| Dynasty / period | Hint |
|------------------|------|
| **Kadambas** | Banavasi — early Kannada polity |
| **Chalukyas of Badami** | Pulakeshin II; cave temples |
| **Rashtrakutas** | Manyakheta; Kailasa Ellora (linked) |
| **Hoysalas** | Belur, Halebidu, Somanathapura |
| **Vijayanagara** | Hampi; Krishnadevaraya; 1565 Talikota |
| **Bahmani / Deccan Sultanates** | North Karnataka |
| **Wodeyars of Mysore** | Kingdom of Mysore |
| **Hyder Ali / Tipu Sultan** | Srirangapatna; Anglo-Mysore wars |
| **Unification movement** | Aluru Venkata Rao and others |

### Geography

- **Western Ghats** (Sahyadri); highest peak in KA: **Mullayanagiri** (Chikkamagaluru)  
- Rivers: **Kaveri, Krishna, Tungabhadra, Sharavathi** (Jog Falls), **Netravati, Kali**  
- Soils: red, black (north), laterite (coast)  
- Coast: **Karavali**; ports: **Mangaluru, Karwar**  
- Wildlife: Bandipur, Nagarahole, Bhadra, Dandeli  
- Districts: know the count from a **current** map (it has changed over years — verify)  

### Economy / culture

- IT capital Bengaluru; public sector: HAL, ISRO centre, etc.  
- Silk, coffee (Chikkamagaluru), sandal, spices  
- Jnanpith Kannada writers (Kuvempu first Kannada) — names rotate in papers  
- Folk: Dollu Kunitha, Veeragase  

### Education / schemes (refresh from official sites)

- Mid-day meal, RTE, Ksheera Bhagya (milk — verify current name)  
- **KARTET**, DSERT, SCERT, textbook society  
- **DIKSHA**, state Vidyagama / ICT programmes (names change — check)
