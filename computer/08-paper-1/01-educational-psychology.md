## 2. Educational psychology

> **Educational psychology** is the study of how humans learn in educational settings — development, learning, motivation, individual differences, and evaluation — to improve teaching.

**Nature:** applied science, positive, developing.  
**Methods:** observation, experiment, case study, introspection (old), survey.

**Need:** understand the child, choose methods, handle problems, evaluate fairly, guide parents.

---

## 3. Growth vs development

| Growth | Development |
|--------|-------------|
| Quantitative (height, weight) | Qualitative + quantitative |
| Stops after a point | Lifelong |
| Physical | Physical, mental, social, emotional, moral, language |

**Principles of development:** continuous; sequential (cephalocaudal: head→toe; proximodistal: centre→out); individual differences; interaction of heredity × environment; development is spiral / proceeds from general to specific.

**Dimensions:** physical, cognitive, language, social, emotional, moral.

**Stages (broad):** infancy, early childhood, later childhood (your GPSTR age), adolescence.

---

## 4. Piaget — cognitive development

Children are little scientists. **Schemas**, **assimilation** (fit new into old), **accommodation** (change schema), **equilibration**.

| Stage | Age (approx) | Hallmark |
|-------|--------------|----------|
| **Sensorimotor** | 0–2 | Object permanence |
| **Pre-operational** | 2–7 | Egocentrism, animism, no conservation |
| **Concrete operational** | 7–11 | Conservation, seriation, **your Class 1–5 / early 6** |
| **Formal operational** | 11+ | Abstract, hypothesis — **Class 6–8 starts here** |

**Teaching:** concrete materials in middle school still help; do not start with pure abstraction.

---

## 5. Vygotsky — social constructivism

- Learning is **social** (language, culture)  
- **ZPD:** zone of proximal development — what the child can do **with help** but not alone  
- **Scaffolding:** temporary help  
- **MKO:** more knowledgeable other (teacher, peer)  

**Teaching:** pair work, hints, not giving the full answer.

---

## 6. Kohlberg — moral development

Levels: **Pre-conventional** (punish/reward), **Conventional** (please others / law), **Post-conventional** (ethics, justice).  
Heinz dilemma is the famous story.

## 7. Learning theories

| Thinker | Theory | CS class use |
|---------|--------|--------------|
| **Pavlov** | Classical conditioning — stimulus pairing | Lab bell → sit (use carefully) |
| **Skinner** | Operant — reinforcement / punishment | Praise correct save-file habit; avoid public shame |
| **Thorndike** | Trial and error; laws of readiness, exercise, effect | Practice typing |
| **Kohler (Gestalt)** | Insight learning | Sudden “aha” in debugging |
| **Bandura** | Social learning / modelling | Teacher types politely; children copy |
| **Bruner** | Discovery; enactive-iconic-symbolic | Do → picture → symbol (flowchart) |
| **Ausubel** | Meaningful reception; advance organiser | Tell the day’s map first |

**Motivation:** intrinsic (curiosity) better than only marks. Maslow: needs hierarchy (safety before self-actualisation) — a hungry/scared child will not learn Python.

## 8. Inclusive education

> **Inclusive education** means all children, including CWSN, learn **together** in the same classroom with support.

- **IEP:** Individualised Education Plan  
- **RPWD Act 2016** — rights of persons with disabilities  
- **RTE 2009** — right to education 6–14  
- Peer tutoring, seating, extra time, simplified notes, ICT (screen readers)  
- **Do not** segregate as “lesser”  

**Gender:** equal lab time. **Multilingual:** allow Kannada explanation of English terms.

---

## 9. Evaluation

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Measurement** | Numbers (18/20) |
| **Assessment** | Gather info |
| **Evaluation** | Judge value against aims |
| **Formative** | During learning (quiz, lab checklist) |
| **Summative** | End (term exam) |
| **CCE** | Continuous and Comprehensive Evaluation — scholastic + co-scholastic |
| **Diagnostic** | Find learning gaps |
| **Remedial** | Repair those gaps |

**Good test:** validity, reliability, objectivity, practicability.

**Bloom’s taxonomy:** Remember, Understand, Apply, Analyse, Evaluate, Create.

---

## 10. Teaching maxims (MCQ favourites)

Known to unknown; simple to complex; concrete to abstract; whole to part; psychological to logical; particular to general.
