# Chapter P1-3 — General Mental Ability

**Paper:** Paper-I Reasoning and Numerical Aptitude  
**Time:** Day 54 + 20 mixed questions every other day  

---

## 1. What they ask

- Number and letter series  
- Coding–decoding  
- Blood relations, directions  
- Analogy, classification (odd one out)  
- Syllogism (some/all/no)  
- Venn, seating (easy)  
- Percent, ratio, average, profit-loss, time-work, time-distance (school level)  
- Data interpretation (small table)

No higher engineering maths. **Speed + accuracy.**

---

## 2. Series

Find the rule: +2, ×2, squares, alternate two series.

Example: 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ?  
Diff 4,6,8,10 → next +12 → **42**

Letters: A C F J O ?  
+2 +3 +4 +5 → +6 → **U**

---

## 3. Coding

If CAT = 24 (3+1+20) know A=1…Z=26.  
If APPLE is coded as BQQMF (+1). Reverse, opposite letters (A↔Z).

---

## 4. Directions

Draw N-E-S-W every time. Right from North = East.  
“Walk 10 m north, 10 m east…” — sketch, do not do in the head.

---

## 5. Blood relations

Make a tiny family tree.  
Mother’s brother = maternal uncle.  
Remember: generation first, then side.

---

## 6. Syllogism (basic)

All A are B; All B are C → All A are C.  
Some A are B; All B are C → Some A are C (usually).  
**No** + **All** needs a Venn. Practise 10 from any TET book.

---

## 7. Numerical short notes

| Topic | Formula |
|-------|---------|
| Average | Sum / n |
| Percentage | (part/whole)×100 |
| Profit % | (profit/CP)×100 |
| Simple interest | PTR/100 |
| Speed | Distance / time |
| Ratio a:b of total T | a/(a+b)×T |

**Work:** If A does in 10 days, 1 day = 1/10.

## 8. Ten practice items (answers below)

1. 5, 10, 20, 40, ?  
2. Odd one: Keyboard, Mouse, Monitor, Windows  
3. If 15% of x = 45, x = ?  
4. Average of 10, 20, 30  
5. CP 200, SP 250, profit %?  
6. All pens are books. All books are papers. All pens are papers? (Y/N)  
7. Face north, turn right, then right: face?  
8. 2, 3, 5, 8, 12, ?  
9. 1/2 + 1/4 = ?  
10. Ratio 2:3, total 50, first part?  

**Answers:** 1) 80  2) Windows (software)  3) 300  4) 20  5) 25%  6) Yes  7) South  8) 17  9) 3/4  10) 20
