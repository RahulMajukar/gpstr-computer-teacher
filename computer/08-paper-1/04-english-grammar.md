# Chapter P1-4 — General English

**Paper:** Paper-I English (about **25 marks**) + helps Paper-III if medium is English  
**Time:** Day 55 + 20 minutes daily in Weeks 7–8  

---

## 1. What to master

- Parts of speech  
- Tenses  
- Articles, prepositions  
- Active / passive  
- Direct / indirect  
- Concord (subject–verb)  
- Degrees of comparison  
- Synonyms, antonyms, one-word  
- Error spotting  
- Unseen passage  

---

## 2. Parts of speech (one example each)

Noun *school*, pronoun *she*, adjective *smart*, verb *teach*, adverb *slowly*, preposition *on*, conjunction *and*, interjection *oh!*

---

## 3. Tenses — form of “teach”

| | Simple | Continuous | Perfect |
|--|--------|------------|---------|
| Present | teach / teaches | is teaching | has taught |
| Past | taught | was teaching | had taught |
| Future | will teach | will be teaching | will have taught |

**Signal words:** now → continuous; yesterday → past; already → perfect.

---

## 4. Articles

- **a** before consonant sound: a computer, a university (yu)  
- **an** before vowel sound: an hour, an LCD  
- **the** unique / known / superlative: the sun, the tallest  

---

## 5. Prepositions (common traps)

interested **in**, good **at**, consist **of**, prefer A **to** B, discuss (no *about* in formal), reach (no *to*), enter (no *into* for a room often), since + point, for + period.

---

## 6. Active ↔ Passive

Active: The teacher **explains** the lesson.  
Passive: The lesson **is explained** by the teacher.

Use *be* + past participle. Same tense family.

---

## 7. Direct ↔ Indirect

Direct: He said, “I am tired.”  
Indirect: He said that he **was** tired.

said **to** → **told**. Questions: if/whether or wh-word; comma and quotes go.

---

## 8. Concord

The list of items **is** on the desk.  
Neither of the boys **is** late.  
The students **are** ready.

---

## 9. Degrees

tall — taller — tallest  
good — better — best  
useful — more useful — most useful  

---

## 10. Passage method

1. Read questions first  
2. Read passage once  
3. Answer in the **passage’s words**, not your story  
4. Vocabulary Q: replace in the sentence and test  

---

## 11. Twenty exam-type items (answers at end)

4. The lesson was ____ by the teacher. (explain/explained)  
5. Opposite of *ancient*: (modern/old)  
6. One word: a person who teaches — ____  
7. They ____ playing now. (is/are)  
8. Prefer tea ____ coffee. (than/to)  
9. He said that he ____ busy. (is/was) — if reporting past  
10. Plural of *child*  
11. ____ Ganga is a river. (A/The)  
12. She has ____ her work. (do/done)  
13. Synonym of *begin*  
14. Error: He go to lab.  
15. Passive of: Open the door.  
16. Comparative of *good*  
17. Preposition: sit ____ the chair. (on/in)  
18. Article: ____ hour  
19. He can ____ English. (speak/speaks)  
20. Antonym of *success*  

**Answers:** 1 goes 2 an 3 since 4 explained 5 modern 6 teacher 7 are 8 to 9 was 10 children 11 The 12 done 13 start/commence 14 goes 15 Let the door be opened 16 better 17 on 18 an 19 speak 20 failure
