# Chapter P1-6 — Health, Physical / Value Education

**Paper:** Paper-I (about **15 marks**)  
**Time:** Day 56  

---

## 1. Why it is in a teacher exam

A primary/upper-primary teacher watches **nutrition, hygiene, first aid, and values**, not only the subject.

---

## 2. Health and hygiene

- **Health:** complete physical, mental, social well-being (WHO idea) — not only “no disease”  
- Personal hygiene: teeth, nails, bath, clean uniform  
- School: toilets, drinking water, mid-day meal hygiene  
- **Balanced diet:** carbs, proteins, fats, vitamins, minerals, water, fibre  
- **Malnutrition:** under and over  
- Deficiency: Vitamin A (night blindness), C (scurvy), D (rickets), Iron (anaemia), Iodine (goitre)  

---

## 3. Diseases

| Type | Spread | Examples | Prevention |
|------|--------|----------|------------|
| **Communicable** | Person / vector / water | Cold, COVID-class, malaria, dengue, typhoid, TB | Hygiene, vaccine, nets, ORS for diarrhoea |
| **Non-communicable** | Not infection | Diabetes, hypertension, some cancers | Diet, exercise, no tobacco |

**Vectors:** mosquito (malaria, dengue).  
**Immunisation:** as per national schedule (names: BCG, DPT, measles — verify current).  
**First aid:** cuts (clean, bandage), burns (cool water, no toothpaste), fractures (do not move roughly), fainting (air, loosen clothes). Know school first-aid box.

---

## 4. Physical education / yoga / aerobics

- Daily physical activity; posture at computers (link to your lab)  
- **Yoga:** asana, pranayama — stress and focus  
- **Aerobics:** oxygen-using exercise (running, jogging)  
- Rest and sleep for children  

**20-20-20 rule** for eyes in the lab: every 20 minutes look 20 feet away for 20 seconds.

---

## 5. Value / moral education

Values: honesty, non-violence, respect, equality, empathy, cleanliness, punctuality, teamwork, patriotism, respect for Kannada and all languages.

**How to teach values:** stories, assembly, teacher as model (Bandura), not only slogans.

**Constitutional values:** justice, liberty, equality, fraternity — link to inclusive class.

**Environmental value:** no e-waste in the drain; save power in the lab.

---

## 6. Child protection (short)

No corporal punishment (RTE). Listen to the child. Report abuse as per school/POCSO duty. Cyber safety (you already have a full chapter).

---

## 7. MCQs

1. WHO health includes: a) only body b) physical mental social c) only marks d) only height  
2. Iron deficiency: a) goitre b) anaemia c) scurvy d) rickets  
3. Dengue vector: a) housefly b) mosquito c) lice only d) none  
4. 20-20-20 is for: a) ears b) eyes c) teeth d) feet  
5. Values are best taught by: a) only punishment b) teacher example + activities c) only exams d) only ICT  

**Answers:** 1-b, 2-b, 3-b, 4-b, 5-b

---

## 8. 5-point revision

Health = WHO whole; diet + hygiene; communicable vs not; first aid + yoga; values by example; lab posture and e-waste.
