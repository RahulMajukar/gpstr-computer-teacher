# Last 4 Days — Full Mock Exam Guide

**When:** Days 57–60 of the 60-day plan  
**Files you need:** your notebook + a timer + blank papers (descriptive)

Official timings can change. Use these **practice timings** unless the admit card says otherwise.

---

## Day 57 — Paper-I style (2 hours 30 minutes)

Write 150 one-mark questions from mixed sources:

- 50 psychology / methods (use Chapter P1-1 headings and invent MCQs, or a TET book)  
- 25 Kannada  
- 25 English  
- 25 GK (10 static Karnataka + 15 current from your news diary)  
- 15 health/values  
- 10 computer literacy (Office, Nudi, OS icons, internet)

**Rules:** OMR discipline — one sitting, no phone, darken circles on a homemade OMR (A,B,C,D boxes).

**After:** mark, write every wrong item in the Mistakes section.

---

## Day 58 — Paper-II Computer Science (3 hours)

### Hour 1 — 50 MCQs (50 marks)

Mix 8–10 from each: Fundamentals, OS, Programming, DBMS, Networks, Office/Web/SE, Pedagogy.

Use the chapter practice files. Time: **60 minutes max**.

### Hours 2–3 — Descriptive (100 marks)

Write **full answers**, not points in your head. Suggested paper:

**3 marks × 6 = 18** (short)

1. RAM vs ROM  
2. Process vs program  
3. Stack vs queue  
4. Primary key  
5. Star topology  
6. Compiler vs interpreter  

**5 marks × 10 = 50**

7. CPU organisation + diagram  
8. Memory hierarchy  
9. Deadlock conditions  
10. Four OOP pillars  
11. 1NF 2NF 3NF  
12. OSI layers  
13. SQL: create student + select marks>60  
14. SDLC stages  
15. Methods of teaching CS  
16. Lab management  

**8–10 marks × 4 = 32** (if your paper uses long Qs, merge two 5-markers)

**Must include diagrams** for 7, 8, 12, and a flowchart or ER if you have time.

Use templates in [02-descriptive-answer-templates.md](02-descriptive-answer-templates.md).

---

## Day 59 — Paper-III language (2 hours)

If **Kannada medium:**

- 1 essay (digital education / cleanliness / Karnataka culture)  
- 1 official letter (lab UPS repair)  
- 1 comprehension  
- Grammar short notes  

If **English medium:** same types in English.

Write on paper, count words, keep 10 minutes to read.

---

## Day 60 — Repair and rest

- Only **Mistakes notebook**  
- Re-draw: CPU, OSI, ER, process states, memory pyramid, 5 topologies  
- Re-write: ACID, Coffman, 4 OOP, 8 OS functions  
- Pack documents mentally: TET, degree, B.Ed, Kannada, photos  
- Sleep 7+ hours  

**Do not start a new thick book on Day 60.**

---

## Scoring honesty

If a descriptive answer has no definition or no diagram when needed, give yourself **half or less**. Examiners do the same.

---
